About Me

I'm a full-stack developer who builds custom software for traditional businesses—the kind that keep the world running but often get overlooked by tech. I specialize in building robust backends using FastAPI, Flask, and SQLAlchemy, paired with reactive React frontends.

Outside of code, I'm a ghostwriter for pop and EDM artists under Dare County. I also run Beautiful's Dream, a YouTube channel making wholesome, educational content for kids.

I believe good software should be like a good mechanic: reliable, honest, and doesn't make you feel stupid for asking questions.
